Required by law

Law

Get the safety data sheet (SDS) for every fragrance oil, and work out the hazard classification of each finished product at your fragrance load.

GB CLP · 1272/2008HSE · hazard label
Law

Where a product is classified, use a CLP label: pictograms, signal word, hazard and precautionary statements, the allergen line, product name, your name, UK address and phone number, and the net quantity.

GB CLP · 1272/2008HSE · hazard label
Law

Make every product traceable: your name and address and a batch or product reference on the product or packaging, with records of what went into each batch.

GPSR 2005 · SI 2005/1803
Law

Give fire-safety warnings with every candle, such as “Never leave a burning candle unattended”. BS EN 15494 sets out the wording and symbols Trading Standards expect.

GPSR 2005 · SI 2005/1803BCF advice pack 2024
Law

Register as self-employed with HMRC once your trading income goes over the £1,000 trading allowance in a tax year.

GOV.UK · trading allowanceGOV.UK · working for yourself
Law

If you sell online or by phone, give buyers the required information before they pay and a 14-day right to cancel. Personalised items are an exception.

SI 2013/3134
Law

Don’t post, buy or swap fake reviews, and don’t show badges or logos you haven’t earned. Both are banned outright.

DMCC Act 2024 · Sch 20

Strongly advised

Advised

Burn-test every wick, wax and fragrance combination and keep the results. BS EN 15493 covers fire safety and BS EN 15426 covers sooting.

BCF advice pack 2024
Advised

Add burner warnings to wax melts: never leave a lit burner unattended, keep it away from children and pets, and don’t move it while the wax is liquid.

GPSR 2005 · SI 2005/1803
Advised

Keep a technical file for each product: SDS, CLP calculation, test results, batch records and supplier invoices.

Advised

Get product liability insurance. It isn’t a legal requirement for sole traders, but most fairs, shops and stockists ask for it.

Advised

Check whether you need to pay the ICO data protection fee. The ICO’s self-assessment takes a few minutes.

ICO · data protection fee

Watch out

Watch

Don’t copy another maker’s CLP label. It only fits their fragrance at their load.

GB CLP · 1272/2008
