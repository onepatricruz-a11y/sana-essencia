Selling online and at markets

The rules that apply to every scent maker, whatever you make.

Checked 7 November 2026

What the law requires

  • Register as self-employed with HMRC once your trading income goes over the £1,000 trading allowance in a tax year. Check GOV.UK for the current figure.

    GOV.UK · trading allowanceGOV.UK · working for yourself

  • Online and phone sales: give buyers the required information before they pay, and a 14-day right to cancel. Personalised items are an exception.

    SI 2013/3134

  • Fake reviews, and showing a trust mark or approval you don’t have, are banned outright. The Competition and Markets Authority can fine businesses directly.

    DMCC Act 2024 · Sch 20CMA207

  • Selling to Northern Ireland or the EU: the EU General Product Safety Regulation applies, including a responsible economic operator based in Northern Ireland or the EU.

    GOV.UK · GPSR in NI

  • Most employers must have employers’ liability insurance from the day they take someone on.

    GOV.UK · employers' liability

Your checklist

  • Business name and contact details on your shop and receipts
  • Delivery, returns and cancellation terms written down
  • Product liability insurance in place (fairs and stockists usually ask for it)
  • Whether you need to pay the ICO data protection fee

Records to keep

  • Sales and expenses for your tax return
  • Copies of your listing wording, with dates
  • Customer complaints and how you resolved them

Mistakes we see most

  • Buying or swapping reviews.
  • Showing logos of bodies you are not a member of.
  • Waiting until the end of the year to register with HMRC.
