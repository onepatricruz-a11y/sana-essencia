Questions and answers

The questions makers ask

Short answers with the law behind them. Search, or read through.

Checked 7 November 2026

Do candles need a CLP label?

Usually, if the scented wax is classified as hazardous. That depends on your fragrance and how much you use, so work it out from your supplier’s safety data sheet (SDS) and a CLP calculation for your exact fragrance load. Many lightly scented candles still need the allergen line.

GB CLP · 1272/2008HSE · hazard labelCandles guide

Can I sell wax melts without a CLP label?

Only if that exact melt is not classified as hazardous. At the fragrance loads most makers use it usually is, so most wax melts need a full CLP label.

GB CLP · 1272/2008Trading Standards · wax meltsWax melts guide

Does handmade soap need a safety assessment?

Yes. Soap is a cosmetic, so each formula needs a Cosmetic Product Safety Report (CPSR) from a qualified safety assessor, a UK Responsible Person and a notification on the government’s SCPN service before you sell it.

UK Cosmetics Reg · 1223/2009GOV.UK · SCPNSoap, bath and body guide

Can I say my candle helps with anxiety or sleep?

No. Saying a product treats or relieves a condition is a medicinal claim. Describe the scent and the mood instead, for example “creates a calm, cosy atmosphere”.

ASA · aromatherapyMHRA · borderline productsClaims checker

What warnings must a candle label show?

Fire-safety warnings such as “Never leave a burning candle unattended” and “Keep out of reach of children and pets”. BS EN 15494 is the standard Trading Standards and the British Candle Makers Federation point to.

BCF advice pack 2024GPSR 2005 · SI 2005/1803Candles guide

Do I need insurance to sell candles?

Product liability insurance is not a legal requirement for a sole trader, but most craft fairs, shops and stockists ask for it. Employers’ liability insurance is compulsory for most businesses once they employ someone.

GOV.UK · employers' liabilitySelling online and at markets guide

Can I make wax melts shaped like sweets?

Avoid it. Products that look like food and could be mistaken for it, especially by children, can break the Food Imitations (Safety) Regulations 1989. Trading Standards have warned wax melt sellers about this.

SI 1989/1291Trading Standards · wax meltsWax melts guide

Do I need to register my candle business?

Register with HMRC as self-employed once your trading income goes over the £1,000 trading allowance in a tax year. There is no special licence to make candles at home, but check your home insurance and any tenancy or mortgage terms.

GOV.UK · trading allowanceGOV.UK · working for yourselfSelling online and at markets guide

Do I need GPSR details to sell on Etsy and other marketplaces?

If you sell to buyers in Northern Ireland or the EU, the EU General Product Safety Regulation applies. You need a responsible economic operator based in Northern Ireland or the EU, and their details must go with the product. Marketplaces ask for this information in your shop settings.

GOV.UK · GPSR in NISelling online and at markets guide

Do reed diffusers need child-resistant caps?

Some do. If your diffuser liquid is classified for certain hazards, such as aspiration hazard (H304), it needs a child-resistant closure and a tactile warning when sold to the public. Section 2 of your SDS tells you.

GB CLP · 1272/2008Reed diffusers guide

Can I call my products non-toxic or chemical-free?

Avoid both. Everything is made of chemicals, and a safety promise like “non-toxic” needs evidence. Say what the product is made of instead.

CMA207Claims checker

Can I say my room spray is antibacterial?

No, unless it is an authorised biocide. Claims that a product kills germs, bacteria or viruses bring it under the biocides rules. “Freshens the room” is fine.

HSE · biocidesRoom and linen sprays guide

Do I need a new CPSR for every scent?

Each formula needs its own safety assessment. Changing the fragrance changes the formula, so the report usually needs updating. Ask your safety assessor how they handle scent variations.

UK Cosmetics Reg · 1223/2009Soap, bath and body guide

Can I use another maker’s CLP label?

No. A CLP label is worked out for one fragrance at one load in one product. Yours has to come from your own SDS and calculation.

GB CLP · 1272/2008HSE · hazard labelCandles guide
