Questions

Is this legal advice?

No. Scent Ledger explains the rules in plain English and links to the law. For advice on your own situation, speak to a solicitor or your local Trading Standards business advice service.

Can I cancel?

Yes, at any time. Monthly plans stop at the end of the month; yearly plans stop at the end of the year.

Do you replace my safety assessor?

No. Cosmetics still need a CPSR from a qualified safety assessor. We help you get everything else right around it.
