Most asked

The questions UK scent makers ask most

Short answers first, then the guide and the law behind each one.

Do candles need a CLP label?

Usually, if the scented wax is classified as hazardous. That depends on your fragrance and how much you use, so work it out from your supplier’s safety data sheet (SDS) and a CLP calculation for your exact fragrance load. Many lightly scented candles still need the allergen line.

GB CLP · 1272/2008HSE · hazard labelRead the candles guide

Can I sell wax melts without a CLP label?

Only if that exact melt is not classified as hazardous. At the fragrance loads most makers use it usually is, so most wax melts need a full CLP label.

GB CLP · 1272/2008Trading Standards · wax meltsRead the wax melts guide

Does handmade soap need a safety assessment?

Yes. Soap is a cosmetic, so each formula needs a Cosmetic Product Safety Report (CPSR) from a qualified safety assessor, a UK Responsible Person and a notification on the government’s SCPN service before you sell it.

UK Cosmetics Reg · 1223/2009GOV.UK · SCPNRead the soap, bath and body guide

Can I say my candle helps with anxiety or sleep?

No. Saying a product treats or relieves a condition is a medicinal claim. Describe the scent and the mood instead, for example “creates a calm, cosy atmosphere”.

ASA · aromatherapyMHRA · borderline productsTry the claims checker

What warnings must a candle label show?

Fire-safety warnings such as “Never leave a burning candle unattended” and “Keep out of reach of children and pets”. BS EN 15494 is the standard Trading Standards and the British Candle Makers Federation point to.

BCF advice pack 2024GPSR 2005 · SI 2005/1803Read the candles guide

Do I need insurance to sell candles?

Product liability insurance is not a legal requirement for a sole trader, but most craft fairs, shops and stockists ask for it. Employers’ liability insurance is compulsory for most businesses once they employ someone.

GOV.UK · employers' liabilityRead the selling online and at markets guide

Can I make wax melts shaped like sweets?

Avoid it. Products that look like food and could be mistaken for it, especially by children, can break the Food Imitations (Safety) Regulations 1989. Trading Standards have warned wax melt sellers about this.

SI 1989/1291Trading Standards · wax meltsRead the wax melts guide

Do I need to register my candle business?

Register with HMRC as self-employed once your trading income goes over the £1,000 trading allowance in a tax year. There is no special licence to make candles at home, but check your home insurance and any tenancy or mortgage terms.

GOV.UK · trading allowanceGOV.UK · working for yourselfRead the selling online and at markets guide

Law updates

When the rules change, it goes in the ledger

Each change is dated, says who it affects and what to do, and links to the law.

EntryDateChangeWho and what to do
No. 00531 Jul 2026

EU expands fragrance allergen labelling for cosmetics

Cosmetics placed on the EU market from this date must name the expanded list of fragrance allergens. Under the Windsor Framework, EU cosmetics rules also apply in Northern Ireland. Great Britain had not adopted the expanded list when we last checked.

Who it affectsSoap, bath and perfume makers selling to Northern Ireland or the EU
What to doAsk your safety assessor which allergens your labels must now list for those markets.
EU 2023/1545
No. 00421 Jul 2025

Product Regulation and Metrology Act 2025 becomes law

Gives ministers powers to update UK product safety rules, including duties for online marketplaces. It changes nothing for makers on its own. The detail will come in regulations made under it.

Who it affectsEveryone selling physical products
What to doNothing yet. We log each regulation made under the Act here.

No. 0036 Apr 2025

New consumer protection rules in force

The Digital Markets, Competition and Consumers Act 2024 bans fake reviews and the display of trust marks or approvals a business doesn’t have. The Competition and Markets Authority can now fine businesses directly.

Who it affectsEveryone selling to consumers
What to doRemove any review you didn’t earn and any badge you aren’t entitled to.
