About Scent Ledger

Scent Ledger is a Sana Essencia product: plain-English UK compliance answers for people who make and sell scented products.

Why it exists

Small makers get compliance advice from forums, suppliers and other makers, and it often disagrees. The rules are public, but they are spread across chemical, product safety, cosmetics and consumer law.

Scent Ledger puts the rules for candles, wax melts, diffusers, sprays, soap and fragrance in one place, in plain English, with the source beside every answer.

Our editorial rules

  • Every answer links to the legislation or official guidance behind it.
  • Every page shows the date we last checked it.
  • Corrections are logged in public, with the date.
  • No paid placements in guidance.
  • Sana Essencia products are never recommended in guidance. Where one is mentioned, it is labelled as ours.
  • A qualified specialist reviews the guidance before full launch.

Who runs it

Scent Ledger is run by Sana Essencia Ltd, a UK company that makes science-led tools for workplaces and scent makers.
