    Team room check

    A 15-minute L&D or team-meeting exercise. Each person rates ten statements from 1 (strongly disagree) to 5 (strongly agree). Enter the team average, then turn the lowest scores into a room plan.

    Team scores

      Facilitator notes
      1. Collect ratings anonymously (paper slips or a quick poll). Report only the team average.
      2. Discuss the two lowest scores first. Ask what makes them low, and what would move them up by one point.
      3. Agree no more than three actions, each with an owner and a date.
      4. Repeat the check in three months and compare.

      The law, explained

      The UK has no single "psychosocial risk" act. The duty comes from general health and safety law, applied through HSE's Management Standards and backed by workplace, equality and harassment law.

      964,000workers with work-related stress, depression or anxiety, 2024/25
      22.1mworking days lost to work-related stress, depression or anxiety
      40.1mworking days lost to all work-related ill health and injury
      £22.9bnannual cost of workplace injury and new ill health, 2023/24
      Source: HSE annual statistics, published 20 November 2025. Great Britain.

      Why clients are asking now

      HSE has made stress and mental wellbeing a business-plan priority and has said it is investigating organisational failures to manage mental health risks, especially where several employees report work-related ill health.

      30 October 2026: harassment duties tighten

      Under the Employment Rights Act 2025, the duty to prevent sexual harassment becomes "all reasonable steps", and employers become liable for harassment of staff by third parties such as customers and contractors. Client-facing rooms need a plan.
