Why scent stays human

AI can suggest a formula. It can’t smell it.

AI is already used in perfumery to sort ingredients and suggest new molecules. Here is what it can’t do, and what this course teaches you to do well.

AI canSuggest a formula

Predict how a molecule might smell, and draft a description or a calendar. Use it for that.

It can’tSmell, pour or test

Notice a blend that feels flat after a week of curing, judge a melt pool or fix a sinkhole.

It can’tHost a room of people

Run a workshop, a hen party or a team day where guests make something with their hands.

Nobody can promise that any work is untouched by technology. These skills depend on a trained nose, skilled hands and hosting people, and they grow with practice.

Questions makers ask

Good to know

Is this an online candle making course for the UK?

Yes. It is an online course written for makers in the UK, so every rule it teaches is UK law, from CLP labels to cosmetic safety reports. It covers candles and wax melts, reed diffusers and room sprays, soap and bath products, and perfume.

Do I need any experience?

No. Module 2 starts with equipment and safety, and every practical lesson goes step by step. If you already make, you can go straight to the business modules.

Will the course tell me how much I will earn?

No. Nobody can honestly promise a figure, and UK advertising rules don’t allow courses to suggest typical earnings without evidence. Module 7 shows you how to work out your own costs and prices, so you can see what your business could make with your numbers.

Do candles need CLP labels in the UK?

Usually, if the scented wax is classified as hazardous. That depends on your fragrance oil and how much you use, so you work it out from your supplier’s safety data sheet. Module 7 shows you how, step by step, and the free Scent Ledger candles guide lists the rules with sources.

Do I need insurance to sell candles?

Product liability insurance is not a legal requirement for a sole trader, but most craft fairs, shops and stockists ask for it. Module 7 explains product and public liability cover, and Module 8 covers insurance for workshops.

Can I sell soap I make at home?

Yes, once each formula has a Cosmetic Product Safety Report from a qualified safety assessor, a UK Responsible Person and a notification on the government’s SCPN service. Module 5 walks you through all three. See the free Scent Ledger cosmetics guide.

How do I start a candle making workshop?

Choose a simple format, write a run sheet, do a risk assessment and get public liability insurance. Module 8 gives you a 90-minute run sheet, pricing, venues and booking platforms. The Workshop Pack adds ready-made run sheets, safety cards and templates.

Is AI going to replace candle and soap makers?

AI is already used in the fragrance industry to suggest formulas and design new molecules. It can’t smell, pour a candle, judge a burn test or host a room of people. Nobody can promise any work is untouched by technology, but these skills depend on a nose, hands and people.

Who makes the course?

The course is produced by Sana Essencia. The making lessons are filmed with an experienced UK maker, who checks every practical step for safety and accuracy. The lessons are narrated by an AI voice.

Is the certificate accredited?

No. You receive a certificate of completion from Sana Essencia. It is not an accredited or regulated qualification, and the certificate says so.

Do you sell candle making kits?

No. Sana Essencia doesn’t sell physical products. Each practical lesson has a kit list with UK suppliers. Some are affiliate links, marked as ads, and we list good options that pay us nothing too.

Module 1 is free now. When sales open, the checkout shows which modules are available on the day you buy and the date each of the others opens, one a week.

Can I cancel or get a refund?

You will see the full terms, including our refund policy, before you pay. For digital content, the 14-day right to cancel ends once you ask to start and confirm you understand that. The law gives you remedies if the course isn’t as described.
