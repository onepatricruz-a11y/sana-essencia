Why scent

The easiest wellbeing habit is the one you breathe.

Scent asks nothing of you. No time, no equipment, no willpower. Smell has a more direct route to the brain’s emotion and memory centres than any other sense, and research suggests some aromas can support alertness and a sense of calm.

For yourselfA small change to your day that takes no time at all.→ For your teamOne easy part of a healthy workplace.→ For someone elseA gift made by people who understand what scent can do.→
