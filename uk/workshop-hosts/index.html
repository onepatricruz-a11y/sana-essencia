Questions studios ask

Good to know

Will other studios see our blends?

No. Your ingredient list and house blends appear only in your studio version.

Who owns the blends we and our guests create?

You do. Blends created in your studio version, by you or your guests, are yours to use under your own booking terms. Sana Essencia claims no rights in them. If you’d like to sell guests’ blends in your own range, add a line to your booking terms and ask before using anyone’s name.

Can we keep selling house blends if we stop?

Yes. House blends we deliver to your studio are yours to keep selling, even if you stop the plan. We may create similar blends for other studios, because common scent pairings belong to no one.

Does it work with fragrance oils and soap?

Not yet. Today the Studio covers essential oils for candles, wax melts, reeds, sprays and perfume. Fragrance oils from your own catalogue, and soap, are next, built with our founding studios and using the limits on your suppliers’ IFRA certificates.

Who keeps our guests’ details?

You do. Guests send their blend to your email, and the host tools keep your workshop plan in your own browser. Sana Essencia doesn’t collect your guest list.

Does it work with our booking system?

Yes, alongside it. It’s a web link and a QR code, so nothing to install and nothing to change in how guests book and pay.

Who is responsible for safety?

You stay responsible for your oils and products. The tools show fragrance loads, flag oils that need care and remind you to check each IFRA certificate and your wax’s maximum load.

What if it isn’t for us?

The founding pilot is free for 3 months with no obligation. After that, you can stop at any time.

Is the blend suggester AI?

Today it matches guests’ words to your ingredients with built-in perfumery rules, so it’s fast and free to run. The AI assistant above comes next.
